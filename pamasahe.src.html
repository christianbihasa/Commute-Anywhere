    LTFRB fare guide, 1 to 50 km

    Fares come straight from the LTFRB general fare guides for traditional PUJs and for aircon modern and electric PUJs, effective September 28, 2026: a minimum fare for the first 4 km, then ₱2.00 or ₱2.40 per added kilometer, rounded to the nearest 25 centavos. Students, seniors and PWDs get 20% off every day, including weekends and holidays. This page counts a partial kilometer as a full one. For clarifications, call the LTFRB 24/7 hotline at 1342.

    Check the taripa. Drivers may charge the new rates only if the new fare matrix is posted inside the jeep. Otherwise the old fare applies, shown under each fare.

    Routes and distances are from the DOTC/LTFRB jeepney route feed released for the Philippine Transit App Challenge, as maintained by Sakay.ph. Distance is measured stop to stop along each route's mapped stops, which are about 250 m apart on average. The feed dates from 2015, so some routes may have changed since. Data provided by DOTC.
